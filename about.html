A Little About Me

Hello, I am Nichita.

Multidisciplinary Art Director with agency background (Ogilvy) and strong illustration skills. My expertise also spans brand design, video editing, 3D modelling and web design.

With over 6 years of experience with international brands such as DOVE, Mastercard, LIDL, Milka and more. I've collaborated with post-production houses, gaining diverse and interesting experiences.

My inspiration comes from all kinds of art, design, culture and music.
